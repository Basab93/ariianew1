Publication & Indexing

All the ARIIA 2026 accepted and presented papers in either Regular or Special Session tracks will be published in the Taylor & Francis Conference Proceedings. All registered and presented papers will be submitted by Taylor & Francis for indexing in major databases including Scopus, EI-Compendex, Web of Science (WoS) and other leading indexing services.
