Call for Reviewers

ARIIA 2026 invites distinguished researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to contribute as reviewers. Your expertise will ensure the quality, integrity, and global impact of the conference proceedings.

Eligibility
  • Strong research background in relevant domains
  • Publications in reputed journals/conferences
  • No conflict of interest
  • Prior reviewing experience preferred
Responsibilities
  • Provide unbiased and constructive reviews
  • Follow double-blind review process
  • Maintain strict confidentiality
  • Submit reviews within deadlines
Apply as Reviewer

Application takes less than 2 minutes

Reviewer Guidelines

Core Ethical Principles
  • Maintain strict confidentiality of submitted manuscripts
  • Ensure impartial and unbiased evaluation
  • Provide scientifically rigorous and evidence-based feedback
  • Adhere to professional and ethical standards
Conflict of Interest

Reviewers must decline assignments if there is any conflict of interest, including:

  • Same institution or recent collaboration
  • Personal or professional relationships
  • Financial or competitive conflicts
Strict Policy: The use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) for generating or modifying review content is strictly prohibited.
Review Process
  • Double-blind peer review system
  • Minimum of three independent reviewers per paper
  • Evaluation based solely on technical merit
Evaluation Criteria
  • Originality and novelty
  • Technical correctness and depth
  • Experimental validation and results
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Relevance to conference themes
Final Recommendation Categories
  • Strong Accept
  • Accept
  • Borderline
  • Reject
  • Strong Reject
